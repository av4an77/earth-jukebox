Each kind of data is an instrument

Desert dust is a warm horn. Smoke from fires is a low growl. Pollution is plucked. Sea salt rings like bells.

More means higher and louder

The stronger the reading, the higher the note. You are playing the atmosphere of 19 August 2025 with your scroll wheel.

How sight becomes sound

In the pictureIn the music
Position, west to eastTime. The needle crosses the picture once every 2 bars
Data value, read from the color scalePitch, kept on a musical scale. The melody moves in small steps so it stays a tune
Kind of data (dust or sea salt, rain or snow)Instrument. The sound key teaches each one
BrightnessLoudness and tone
Change between framesPercussion. Sudden flashes become thunder
The whole frameThe chord and bass underneath
Left and right, top and bottomStereo position and register

Values are estimated from each picture’s published color scale, not from the raw science files.
